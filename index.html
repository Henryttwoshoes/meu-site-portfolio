About Me

World leading D&D Scientist and map maker (probably).

In 2022 I graduated with a Masters in Astrophysics and panicked. In my panic, I decided to combine my love of D&D 5e and data analysis and create The Theory of Magic. Since then, I have written more and more 5e science analysis that has been given such reviews as:

"Well that's interesting", "People buy this S***?", and "Magic? Like card tricks?"

I also make maps for my games and share them here when I can.

Thank you everyone for your support, patience, and just general good will. If you want to support my work, feel free to go to my patreon (the leftmost button).
